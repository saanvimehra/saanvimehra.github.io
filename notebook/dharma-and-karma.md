I grew up in [[Growing up in Noida|Noida]], a suburb of Delhi, India, with a strong sense of [धर्म](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dharma) and [कर्म](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karma) shaped by my parents, the two most principled and hardworking people I know.

## धर्म · Dharma

Duty, ethics, and right action.

## कर्म · Karma

Action, work, or deed and its consequences.

In [[lucky-13|Lucky #13]], I wrote about my family's fast on Trayodashi, the thirteenth day of the lunar cycle.
