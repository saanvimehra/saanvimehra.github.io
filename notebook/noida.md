I grew up in Noida, a suburb of Delhi, India. I have a sister 6 years younger than me, who has taught me more about humanity than everything, everyone else, combined.

My favourite colour is green. I use British English because I studied in the particularly colonially influenced ICSE board. It has caused more bugs than I'd liked to admit because of my insistence on seeing the letters *our* together.
