Reading / Poem

English Girl Eats Her First Mango

John Agard

taste this mango

The speaker has a whole speech ready. What comes out is three words. That little change of register is the invitation at the entrance to this tree.

Read the poem

The cilantro in my letter to a future roommate does something similar. A conversation about food becomes a way of getting to know someone.
