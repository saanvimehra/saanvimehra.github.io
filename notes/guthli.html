A guthli is the hard stone inside a fruit. In this typeface it became the dot above the i in Saanvi. Look closely at the h in Mehra as you move the slider. The feet grow farther on one side than the other.

Guthli is a custom cut of Josh Puckett’s Allo, which also credits the Inter Project. It has one upright variable cut, with a continuous serif axis. The font files and editable build recipe are included in the download.

On the tree, Guthli sets my name. Nyght Serif sets the headings and Onest carries the running text.
