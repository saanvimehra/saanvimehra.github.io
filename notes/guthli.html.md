# Guthli

Typeface · Nov 2026

A *guthli* (गुठली) is the hard stone inside a fruit. In this typeface it became the dot above the **i** in Saanvi.

Guthli borrows a motion from the curved strokes in गुठली. The stroke carries weight into a bend and comes out lighter. The lower loop of the **g** turns back to the left. The **t** has a smaller turn at its foot.

The **g** has a small eye above a wide lower loop. It takes up less room than the open **g** in the alternate cut.

Guthli is a custom cut of [Josh Puckett’s Allo](https://github.com/joshpuckett/allo), which also credits the Inter Project. The **a**, **e**, **g**, **m**, **p**, **r** and **t** have their own drawings. The other letters build on Allo’s outlines. Both cuts have a serif slider. At its lowest setting you can still see a small foot on the **r**. The download includes the fonts and the editable build recipe.

Guthli 1.0 sets the type across this website. The download includes both Guthli and Guthli Open.
