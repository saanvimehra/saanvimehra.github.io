About me

  • Studying at Penn

    I'm a student in the M&T program at Penn, where I study finance @ Wharton and CS Eng @ Penn Engineering. Set Theory was one of the first mathematical concepts I was exposed to. I realised pretty early on that I love intersection sets. As a result, I've always sought problems that simulates multiple dimensions of who I am, sitting across agentic payments, stablecoin research, credit scoring, insurance infrastructure in India, and sensitivity optimization in multimodal ML and computer vision systems.

    I'm a student in the M&T program at Penn where I study finance and computer science. I'm interested in figuring out the intersections sets that exist in the world.

  • Working in payments

    I'm currently working at Natural as an Engineer. In the past, I built Orbete, a credit layer based on stablecoin transaction behavior, and worked at Stripe to better understand how payments reach meaningful scale. I've also spent time with Blockchain Capital, Bullish, Zopper, Penn Blockchain, the Wharton Innovation Fund, and Wharton's Stevens Center Fintech Fellowship.
