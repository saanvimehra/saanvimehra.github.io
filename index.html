I'm a student in the M&T program at Penn, where I study finance @ Wharton and CS Eng @ Penn Engineering. Set Theory was one of the first mathematical concepts I was exposed to. I realised pretty early on that I love intersection sets. As a result, I've always sought problems that simulates multiple dimensions of who I am, sitting across agentic payments, stablecoin research, credit scoring, insurance infrastructure in India, and sensitivity optimization in multimodal ML and computer vision systems.

I'm currently working at Blockchain Capital as a research scholar. In the past, I built Orbete, a credit layer for crypto based on stablecoin transaction behavior, and worked at Stripe to better understand how payments reach meaningful scale. I've also spent time with Bullish, Zopper, Penn Blockchain, the Wharton Innovation Fund, and Wharton's Stevens Center Fintech Fellowship.
